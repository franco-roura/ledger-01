# Code of conduct

Ledger/01 is a small project, and we want it to be a friendly place to take part in.

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in any form.
- Don't share anyone's private information, including their financial data.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and block
people who keep breaking them.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are kept private.
